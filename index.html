For

,

I’m sorry.

I know those two words cannot undo the hurt I caused, but I need you to know that they come from the deepest, most honest part of me.

You mean far too much to me for pride to stand between us. I should have think more carefully, spoken more gently, and speak you in the way in that moment.

I cannot promise to be perfect. I can promise to try, to do better, and to keep choosing not only when it is easy, but especially when it is not.

If you need time, I understand. I just hope that, when you are ready, you will let me make things right.

Always yours,
with all my heart
