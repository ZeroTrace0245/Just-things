OUR LITTLE MAGIC

one month of us

A tiny letter from
the person who adores you

For Byeole
my favourite person
Somewhere under the same moon
With a heart that is very full

My dearest Byeole,

Happy one month, my love. Before anything else, I want to say I am sorry for the mistakes I have made along the way.

I know that saying sorry does not erase the moments when I hurt you, disappointed you, or made things harder than they needed to be. You deserved more patience, care, and understanding from me, and I am taking responsibility for that.

I am still learning, but I want to learn beside you. I will listen better, communicate more honestly, and show you through my actions that your feelings matter to me. Thank you for giving us room to grow.

Even with my imperfect moments, this first month with you has meant so much to me. I am grateful for every laugh, every conversation, and every ordinary moment that became a memory. I love you, and I want to keep choosing you with more kindness every day.

Always yours,
Your secret admirer
